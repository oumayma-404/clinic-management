# Clinic Management — Your Complete Practice, in One Place

A modern management solution for dental and medical clinics, designed for the way clinics
in Tunisia actually work. French interface, Tunisian governorates, and everything your team
needs to run the practice — from the first appointment to the final invoice.

Runs **online in the cloud** or **fully offline on your own clinic computer/network** — your choice.

---

## Why Clinics Choose It

- **Everything in one place** — patients, appointments, records, documents, files, stock, and billing details, all connected.
- **Works with or without internet** — keep working even when the connection drops; the app reconnects on its own.
- **Built for teams** — doctors, secretaries, and administrators each get the right access, and everyone stays in sync in real time.
- **Multiple clinics, one system** — each clinic's data stays completely private and separate.
- **In French, for Tunisia** — familiar language, local address formats, and Tunisian governorates throughout.

---

## The Features

### 🏠 Dashboard — Your Day at a Glance
Open the app and immediately see what matters: **live activity figures** for the day and week,
and **today's appointment schedule** front and center. No digging required.

---

### 👥 Patient Records
The heart of the clinic — a complete, searchable file for every patient.

- **Full patient profiles**: name, date of birth, gender, contact details, and address with **Tunisian governorate**.
- **Insurance details**: provider, policy and group numbers, and expiry date.
- **Medical & family history**: add, update, and remove entries over time.
- **Allergies and clinical notes** kept alongside the file.
- **Patient alerts/flags** — mark patients who need special attention, and filter to see only flagged patients instantly.
- **Powerful search** to find any patient in seconds.

---

### 🦷 Dental Charting
Professional tooth-by-tooth clinical charting built for dentists.

- **Interactive visual dental chart** for both **adults and children** (primary teeth).
- Record each intervention: **date, procedure, affected teeth, and per-tooth notes**.
- **Track payments** — cost of the procedure and amount already paid.
- Full history of dental work per patient.

---

### 📅 Appointments & Calendar
Scheduling that's fast, visual, and never double-books.

- **Day and week calendar** with clear hourly slots — click to book or open an appointment.
- Book appointments with **patient, doctor, procedure type, duration, and notes** — or block a slot as simply "busy."
- **Reschedule, cancel, or delete** with confirmation, so nothing happens by accident.
- **Filter** by date, doctor, or patient.
- **Automatic reminders** appear in your team's notification feed ~24 hours before each appointment.
- **Google Calendar integration** — appointments you create are pushed straight to Google Calendar, with a clear indicator showing what's synced and a one-tap "push to Google" for anything pending.

---

### 📄 Medical Documents
Generate polished, professional clinical documents in a few clicks — ready to print or send as PDF.

- **Ordonnance** (prescription)
- **Lettre de liaison** (referral letter to a colleague or specialist)
- **Note d'honoraires** (itemized fee note / invoice)
- **Certificat médical** (medical certificate / justification)

Each document is tied to the patient and automatically carries your **clinic and doctor details**,
renders to a **clean, professional PDF**, and can be **downloaded and saved** to the patient's file.

---

### 🗂️ Files & Attachments
A digital filing cabinet for every patient.

- Attach **any document or image** — X-rays, scans, consent forms, lab results — to a patient.
- **Organize with folders**, upload with descriptions, **preview, download, and delete**.
- Browse files **per patient** or across the **whole clinic** in one place.

---

### 📋 Medical Records Browser
Quickly browse across all patients and open a clean, read-only summary — patient details and dental
history at a glance — perfect for a fast lookup.

---

### 🩺 Procedure Catalog
Set up the procedures your clinic offers once, and reuse them everywhere.

- Define each procedure with a **name, typical duration, default cost, color, and description**.
- Used automatically when booking appointments and recording treatments — consistent every time.

---

### 📦 Stock & Inventory
Never run out of supplies mid-treatment.

- Track every consumable: **quantity on hand, minimum/maximum levels, unit, price, and supplier**.
- **Low-stock alerts** appear automatically in your notifications — click one to jump straight to the item.
- **Filter to low-stock items** to know exactly what to reorder.

---

### 🔔 Notifications
A live activity feed keeps the whole team informed.

- A **bell with an unread counter** in the header.
- See the newest updates — **new bookings, reminders, cancellations, reschedules, and low-stock alerts** — with clear icons and timestamps.
- **Mark as read** individually or all at once.
- **Click any notification** to jump directly to the relevant appointment or stock item.
- Updates appear **in real time** across the team.

---

### 🤖 AI Assistant
A helpful assistant available on every screen, ready to chat and get things done.

- **Ask questions in plain language** — and even **speak** using voice input.
- The assistant can **take real actions** for you:
  - Book an appointment
  - Find and open a patient's record
  - List upcoming appointments
  - Cancel an appointment

It's like having an extra pair of hands at the front desk.

---

### ⚙️ Clinic Settings
Make the app your own.

- **Clinic profile**: name, address with governorate, phone, email, and **your logo**.
- **Manage your doctors**: names, specialties, and contact details.
- **One-click backup** (offline installs) — save your entire database and all files to a folder of your
  choice, with confirmation of exactly where it was saved.

---

### 👤 Staff & Access Control
Give each team member the right level of access.

- **Three roles**: Doctor, Secretary, and Administrator.
- **User management** (administrators): see who's active, their last login, add staff, **reset passwords**
  (with a one-time temporary password), and **deactivate or reactivate** accounts.
- **Secure clinic join code** — new staff join with a code you control and can regenerate anytime.

---

### 🏥 Multiple Clinics, Total Privacy
Run several clinics on the same system with complete confidence — **each clinic's patients,
appointments, documents, and data are fully separated**. Staff only ever see their own clinic.

---

### 🌐 Works Online or Offline
- Runs as a **cloud service**, or **fully offline on your own clinic computer and network** — no internet required.
- A **connection indicator** always shows your status.
- Internet-dependent features step aside gracefully when you're offline and **switch back on automatically** when you reconnect.
