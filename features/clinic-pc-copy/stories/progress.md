## Learnings

- A lease measured on « time since the last ack » alone confuses a busy or sleeping PC with a dead cloud. Require that the PC asked and got no answer, and keep a heartbeat beside any long piece of work.
- A probe that checks « is this refused? » on a door FR-11 keeps open really writes. Send account writes to the PC only.
- A job that SENDS before it saves cannot be protected by a save-level net alone: the net refuses the record, not the message. Ask before acting.
- This laptop sleeps during long waits (Kernel-Power 42): a log gap on both servers at once is the machine, not the product.
- A Playwright-launched Chrome window behind other windows gets its animation frames throttled, so every click waits on « element is not stable » for ever: launch with `--disable-backgrounding-occluded-windows --disable-renderer-backgrounding --disable-background-timer-throttling`.
- Closing the only page of a Playwright persistent context closes Chrome with it — to stop a page's loop, navigate it to `about:blank`.
- `localhost` reaches the scratch cloud over IPv6 for one client and IPv4 for another: bind the scratch cloud to `127.0.0.1` when a check compares callers' addresses.
- On Windows, `File.Move(tmp, path, overwrite: true)` over a file written milliseconds earlier is refused with « Access denied » (antivirus / indexer hold) — hit by 12 follower tests at once. `Relay/AtomicFile` retries the move; any repeated small-file write on the PC needs it.
