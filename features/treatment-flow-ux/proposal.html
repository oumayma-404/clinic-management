The treatment flow, made obvious

RDV with a multi-séance act → its fiches de soins → the treatment page. UI only: every server rule from the 4 waves stays, and no capability is removed (see Nothing is lost).

1 · One word per ideaTraitement · Séance · Payé · Reste à payer. Nothing else.
2 · Facts in bold, no instructionsA figure + 3 words replaces a sentence.
3 · One séance strip, everywhereFaite · prévue · à planifier. Same look on every screen, and you edit by tapping it.
Today: 7 ways to show progress 25 explanation texts in the fiche 21 dialogs on the treatment page, longest ≈120 words the same balance under 4 names

Approach

3 ways to do it, challenged
ApproachVerdictWhy
Only cut the text⚠ PartialQuick, but the flow stays hard to follow: 7 progress displays, edits spread over 21 dialogs.
Séance strip as the spine + facts in bold✅ RecommendedOne component on 4 screens. Each séance is edited where it is shown. No server change.
Step-by-step wizard❌ RiskyIt locks doctors into one order, and they refine as they go (« the app should not block edits »).
